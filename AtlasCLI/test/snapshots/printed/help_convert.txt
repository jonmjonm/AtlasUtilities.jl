$ mapatlas convert --help


  convert <args> [options] [flags]



Args

  <src>                                                     the atlas to convert (.jsonl, .jsonl.gz,
                                                            or .jsonl.bz2).

  <dst>                                                     the converted atlas to write (.jsonl,
                                                            .jsonl.gz, or .jsonl.bz2).



Options

  --to <version>                                            the format version to write, 1 or 2 (default: 2).



Flags

  --quiet                                                   do not print the number of maps converted.

  -h, --help                                                Print this help message.
[exit 0]
